These are flagged as confirmed defects for downstream AI Failure Classification (Test Healer), not as test-design errors.
- Open Questions (OQ1, OQ3, OQ5, OQ6, OQ7, OQ8, OQ9) that have no resolving scenario in the Structured Test Plan are **not** fabricated into test cases; per rule, undocumented behavior is never assumed. Where a test case touches an area with an open, unresolved question, this is called out in its Description.
